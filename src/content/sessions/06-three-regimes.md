The last lab before the mid-semester break, and the one that decides whether
your Cultural Case Study is an essay about a place or an argument about a
question. That difference is most of the mark, and it is much cheaper to fix
now than in week 8.

## Before the lab

- Bring the culture you chose in week 5, and whatever you have read since.
  "Nothing yet" is an acceptable answer this week and will not be in the next
  one.
- Have your Nap Log ready to submit. The recording period is finished; if the
  analysis is not, come anyway and use the last part of the session.

## In the lab

**Mapping, thirty minutes.** Two axes on the board — scheduled to unscheduled,
private to public. Each group places siesta, wǔxiū and inemuri, then the
document they brought last week. The arguments start immediately and they are
the point: inemuri is the one that splits a room, because sleeping in a meeting
is maximally public and yet nobody scheduled it.

**Your regime, added.** You place your chosen culture on the same axes in front
of people who do not know it. If you cannot place it, you do not yet know what
your case study is about, and finding that out today is the best outcome
available in this session.

**Question, not country, twenty-five minutes.** In pairs, you turn "I am writing
about Spain" into something answerable. A question has a form like *what does a
person have to be seen to be, in order to sleep in this setting, and who decided
that?* Your partner's job is to refuse anything that could be answered by an
encyclopaedia entry. Be strict with each other; the marker will be.

**Nap Log clinic, remaining time.** Bring your analysis and ask about it. The
most common failure is a fortnight of careful entries followed by three
sentences of summary — the entries are the evidence, and the analysis is the
thing being assessed.

## Afterwards

The [Nap Log](/assessments/) is due this week. Submit it before the break rather
than during it: the analysis benefits from being written while the fortnight is
still recent.

Over the break, read a first-hand account from your chosen culture — a diary, an
interview, a complaint, a set of workplace rules. You need one voice from inside
the arrangement. Week 7 picks the course back up on who is permitted to be seen
sleeping, which is your case study's question in a different setting.
